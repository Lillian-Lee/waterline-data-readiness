Can these water figures be reported together?

A water services analyst may receive pipe records and organisation-wide measures from different sources. This review checks what each source says and whether the figures can be compared.

What this means for reporting

This sample can describe the 25 pipe records, but it cannot tell us about every pipe in the network. The source's criticality field is blank in all 25 rows. Tararua's four selected workbook values are present, but the workbook does not give confidence ratings for them.

One file describes individual pipes. The other reports measures for each water service organisation. There is no verified link showing which pipes are counted in each organisation's figures, so report them separately.

For a water services team, these checks help prevent a small sample being mistaken for a whole-network result. Ask the data owners to clarify the blank fields before relying on them. This review does not assess pipe safety, engineering needs or investment priority.

Why this is relevant to Waitī Waters

In its public October 2026 update, Waitī Waters said it had requested council data files to assess data quality and the work needed to clean and move information into new systems. It also described developing measures for internal performance and external reporting. This review uses public data, but demonstrates a related task: checking definitions and gaps before reporting figures.

What we found in the pipe sample

  • These are the first 25 in-use records sorted by ID, not a random sample. The findings apply only to these records.
  • All 25 rows include a condition grade and installation date.
  • The source's criticality field is blank in all 25 rows. The data owner should explain what the field means and where its values are kept.
  • The sample contains 2,829.40 metres of pipe. This is not the total length of the network.
  • The source does not explain its material codes or condition-grade scale, so this report leaves them as supplied.
Missing rating in the workbook: Tararua has values for all four selected measures, but no data confidence ratings are shown. No rating was supplied, and none has been guessed.

Questions for the data owners

  • What do the material codes and condition grades mean?
  • Where is the criticality information held, and how is it defined?
  • How were the organisation-wide measures calculated, and what do the confidence ratings mean?
